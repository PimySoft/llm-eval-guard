# Billing

## Payment methods

Acme Cloud Drive accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express and PayPal. Business plans can also
pay by bank transfer on annual invoices.

## Refund policy

Annual subscriptions can be refunded in full within 14 days of purchase. Monthly subscriptions
are not refundable, but you can cancel any time and keep access until the end of the billing
period.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, we retry three times over 7 days. After the final retry fails, the account
is moved to read-only mode until the payment method is updated.

## Invoices

Invoices are emailed to the account owner on every charge and can be downloaded as PDF from
Settings > Billing > Invoices.
