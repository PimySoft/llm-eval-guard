# Security

## Encryption

Files are encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication (2FA) is available on all plans with an authenticator app. SMS codes
are available on Pro and Business only. Business admins can require 2FA for every member.

## Account recovery

If you lose your 2FA device, use one of the ten single-use recovery codes generated at setup.
Without a recovery code, support verifies your identity, which takes up to 3 business days.

## Data residency

Business customers can choose to store data in the EU (Frankfurt) or US (Virginia) region. Free
and Pro data is stored in the US region.
